# Muscimol: The Untapped Potential of Neural Inhibition

An interactive English-language neuroscience literature presentation, prepared 17 November 2026.

## Open
- Standalone HTML: `Muscimol_The_Untapped_Potential_Interactive.html` (the separately delivered single-file version, with all pictures and scripts embedded).
- Editable project: open `index.html` in any modern browser. The accompanying `style.css`, `app.js`, and `assets/` directory should stay beside it.

## Navigation
Scroll or use arrow/page keys; click chapter names. On mobile use Chapters menu. Fullscreen available. Choose perturbation experiments in the interactive network schematic. Search bibliography by title, author or DOI.

## Bibliographic method
The citations were checked against publicly available publisher or PubMed metadata. Every scientific publication has a DOI link. One FDA safety guidance item is not a journal article and has an official agency link. The presentation distinguishes published experimental findings from research proposals.

## Visual assets
The Amanita and Psilocybe-like illustrative images are AI-generated and do not claim taxonomic identification. No third-party photograph is included. These are illustrative figures, not depictions of an actual experimental preparation.

## Technical
HTML/CSS/JavaScript, no frameworks or build step. The single-file presentation works offline (remote Google Fonts are optional and fall back to installed fonts). Academic DOI pages require a network connection.
